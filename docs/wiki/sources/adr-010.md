# ADR 010 — Deletion-only simplifier

[Raw source](../../raw/adr/010-simplifier-stage.md)

After implementation, a distinct-model simplifier may only remove work not required by criteria. It may not remove criterion-covering tests. The lead reruns verification after simplification.
